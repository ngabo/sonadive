Services used: AI & Automation, Financial Analytics, Data Engineering

27%Reduction in default rates across consumer loan portfolio
40%Improvement in model accuracy vs legacy scoring system

A B2B SaaS company was losing 8% of customers monthly with no early warning system in place. We built a churn prediction model using product usage signals, support ticket patterns, and engagement metrics, then integrated it into their CRM workflow.

Services used: AI & Automation, Product Analytics, Data Engineering

34%Reduction in monthly churn rate within 90 days
89%Model accuracy in identifying at-risk accounts 30 days early
